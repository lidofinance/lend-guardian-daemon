# Benchmark results

> **Pre-release figures.** They were measured on a pre-release version of the SDK and the daemon. Later versions, contract upgrades, chain activity and provider limits can change them; they are not guarantees.

The latest measurements of the SDK and the daemon. The benchmark suite ran on a developer laptop against synthetic nodes that a separate local process serves over loopback HTTP, so its figures are indicative. The daemon image ran in a container with the 2 CPUs and 4 GB that [the daemon guide](../daemon/README.md#requirements) requires, against local nodes. A live network adds round trips, provider limits and load. [README.md](README.md) describes the method.

## Benchmark suite

The median of five runs of the `full` preset, and one run of each `liveness` scenario, whose capacity search uses chain time only and repeats alike.

| Family           | Scenario                                                                                                           | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `discovery`      | 10,000 supplies in the discovery window                                                                            | 1.1 s of CPU, 107 RPC requests, peak 327 MB                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `reconstruction` | 5,000 positions                                                                                                    | 5.9 s of CPU, 30,018 RPC requests, peak 402 MB                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `screening`      | 10,000 anchors                                                                                                     | 2.1 s of CPU, 10,009 RPC requests, peak 443 MB                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `reports`        | 1,000 targets, each reported as suspect and flagged, in batches of 50                                              | attested with 41 transactions in 100 of the 246-block budget                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `cycle`          | first cycle over 5,000 positions                                                                                   | attested; 6.5 s of CPU, 35,030 RPC requests, peak 423 MB                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `cycle`          | the same positions after a heartbeat, nothing due                                                                  | 114 RPC requests                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `liveness`       | due positions, all suspect, whose first cycle attests within the budget, reads from the modelled provider included | 1,340 in batches of 20 with two confirmations between batches; 2,035 in batches of 50; 1,860 with one confirmation; 2,450 with both, as the daemon sends; 2,200 when screening takes the 290 s the daemon waits at most; 19,643, or at least 20,000 with that screening time, when up to five replans of a stale plan reuse the stale attempt's work and keep the suspects it recorded, as the daemon replans |
| `transport`      | 2,000 requests, 50 at once, over loopback HTTP                                                                     | 5.8 ms at the median, 12.6 ms at the 95th percentile                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `anvil`          | observe cycle over 1,000 supplies on the pinned contracts                                                          | 1.1 s of CPU, 4,021 HTTP attempts, peak 336 MB                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `anvil`          | signal cycle over 1,000 suspect supplies                                                                           | attested with 21 transactions in 77 blocks                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `daemon`         | observe cycle over 1,000 anchors                                                                                   | peak 402 MB, against 312 MB for the SDK alone; screening request 502 KB; largest event 446 KB                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `daemon`         | the same cycle with a receiver that answers each post in one second                                                | peak 636 MB with 3,856 posts open at once                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

In the `reports` row the reads before the first report take no chain time. The `liveness` row first reads every position from a node modelled on a paid shared provider, as [README.md](README.md#method) describes. Against the previous measurement, reconstruction reads one more block per position, the check that its newest event sits in the canonical block, and the heartbeat is preceded by a few reads per report kind and per recorded suspect; the replan capacity rose because a repeated attempt keeps the suspects it already recorded instead of sending them again. The search for the replan capacity without screening delay found 19,643 and could not probe beyond it, because the synthetic chain answers a heartbeat past the window with a revert rather than with the aged-out stop the search expects.

## Daemon image

A container with 2 CPUs, 4 GB of memory and a 3 GB heap on the same laptop, posting every event to a receiver. The stands that ran these cycles are not part of this repository.

Against Anvil with the pinned contracts and a history of a few hundred blocks, one run per row:

| Run                                                  | Result                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Observe cycle over 1,000 lots                        | observed in about 2 s; 3,014 RPC requests and one screening request; peak 322 MB |
| Signal cycle over the same lots, every tenth flagged | two flag transactions of 50 and the heartbeat confirmed; peak 329 MB             |
| Node's heap limit in the 4 GB container              | 3,264 MB with `--max-old-space-size=3072`; 2,240 MB without it                   |

Against a synthetic node that serves the benchmark's history, observe cycles only. Each position holds one lot that is due, and the lots are spread over the whole seven-day window, so a cycle sends about six requests per position, against three on Anvil's short history. The screening service answers at once and judges every position clean. The node answers each request at once, after 50 ms, or with delays modelled on a paid shared provider: 2 s for a log read that finds nothing, 0.4 s for one that returns logs and 50 ms for any other request. CPU time is the container's, including a probe that reads its counters every second.

| Positions | Node's delay per request | Cycle time  | CPU time   | RPC requests | Peak memory |
| --------- | ------------------------ | ----------- | ---------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| 1,000     | none                     | about 3.5 s | about 4 s  | 6,104        | 350 MB      |
| 5,000     | none                     | about 15 s  | about 18 s | 30,014       | 433 MB      |
| 1,000     | 50 ms                    | 47 s        | 12 s       | 6,104        | 324 MB      |
| 5,000     | 50 ms                    | 3.8 min     | 51 s       | 30,014       | 374 MB      |
| 1,000     | modelled provider        | 14.9 min    | 32 s       | 6,103        | 254 MB      |

Three cycles in a row over 5,000 positions peaked between 408 and 433 MB, and no second of these cycles kept more than about 1.5 CPUs busy.
